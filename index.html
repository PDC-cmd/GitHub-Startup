Working Camera

A mini game that teaches the basics of photography by letting you take the shot.

Play the prototype

Research Question

How would I make this? How will I make it work? What steps am I going to take when making it?

Why it matters

I want people to get into art, and I believe photography is a really good place to start, since everyone has a phone with a camera on it. Just learning the basics of photography will help people, whether they go further with it or not.

Notes and Experiment Log

November 19, 2026 · First playable prototype

What I tried
Building the whole idea as one web page: four photography paths (Portrait, Landscape, Action, Night) with three assignments each. You set aperture, shutter speed and ISO, drag to frame the picture, press the shutter, and get one to three stars with feedback on what your settings did.
Notes
The scenes are simple drawings, not real photos. The second pass added Snap the camera mascot, ranks with a progress bar, assignments that unlock in order, and an album of your best photos.
Still to check
The prototype has not been play-tested yet. I need to play through all twelve assignments and note anything that is broken, confusing or too hard.
Next step
Watch a few people who know nothing about cameras try it, and fix whatever confuses them.
